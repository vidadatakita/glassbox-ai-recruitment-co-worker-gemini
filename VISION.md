# Vision

## Hiring is everyday work that decides everything else

Every organization depends on the people it hires, yet hiring is one of the least supported workflows in the
company. The knowledge of what a good hire looks like sits in a few people's heads. Decisions are made under time
pressure, and the reasons are rarely written down.

We believe AI can change that, not by replacing the people who hire, but by working beside them.

## What we are building toward

**A co-worker for every hiring decision.** It reads, organizes and explains, so that recruiters spend their time on
judgement instead of paperwork, and every decision can be explained afterwards.

## Three principles

1. **Gemini suggests; people decide.** The AI proposes at every step. A named person chooses, and that choice is
   recorded. Nothing moves a candidate forward or rejects them automatically.
2. **Every recommendation has its evidence.** A score or a suggestion without a reason behind it is not shown.
3. **The AI never sees what it must not judge.** Personal details that should not influence a hiring decision are kept
   away from the model.

## How this fits the Future of Work

| The track asks for | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Access knowledge | The organization's own understanding of each role, captured once, reviewed by people, and reused |
| Collaborate | Recruiters, interviewers and owners working from one shared record |
| Make decisions | A clear recommendation with reasons at each step, and a person who decides |
| Complete everyday work | Drafting, reading and summarising done in minutes instead of days |
| Productivity and efficiency | Less time reading, faster answers for candidates |

## Where we start

Indonesia, where many growing companies hire at volume with small HR teams. The same need exists everywhere.
