# Responsible AI

Hiring decisions change people's lives. These are the commitments the product is built on.

## Our commitments

1. **A person makes every decision.** The AI recommends. A named person decides, at every step, and the decision is
   recorded with their name.
2. **No decision by the AI alone.** No candidate is moved forward or rejected automatically.
3. **Protected information stays out.** Details such as name, photo, age, gender, religion and marital status are not
   used by the AI to assess a candidate.
4. **Explainable by default.** Every recommendation states its reasons, so a person can agree or disagree with it.
5. **Respect for candidates.** Candidates are told where they stand and what happens next, and they receive an answer.
   They are never shown a ranking against other people.
6. **Consent and privacy.** Candidates are told what is collected, why, and for how long, before they apply.
7. **Synthetic data in this project.** Everything in this repository and its demo is invented.

## What we do not claim

We do not claim the system is free of bias. We design it so that people stay accountable, reasons stay visible, and
the AI is kept away from information it must not judge.
